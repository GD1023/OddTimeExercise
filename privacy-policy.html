Privacy Policy

OddTime  ·  Geetansh Dhuria

✅

OddTime does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Nothing leaves your device — ever.

What data does OddTime collect?

None. OddTime collects no personal information of any kind.

There are no user accounts, no sign-ups, and no forms that ask for your name, email, or any other identifying details (other than a first name you optionally enter within the app, which stays on your device).

Where is my data stored?

Everything OddTime saves — your exercise history, preferences, favorites, and streak — is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device.

If you delete the app, all of this data is deleted with it.

Does the app connect to the internet?

No. OddTime makes no network requests. It works fully offline and does not communicate with any server, API, or cloud service.

Does the app use analytics or tracking?

No. OddTime contains no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting tools, no advertising frameworks, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

  • No Google Analytics
  • No Firebase
  • No Facebook SDK
  • No Mixpanel or Amplitude
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA)

Does the app access my camera, microphone, or location?

No. OddTime does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, calendar, GPS location, or any other device sensor or permission.

The word "location" in the app (At My Desk, On the Couch, etc.) refers to a situation you manually select — it is not GPS or location services.

What about notifications?

OddTime can send local reminder notifications if you choose to enable them in Settings. These reminders are scheduled entirely on-device and do not involve any server or third-party service. You can turn them off at any time from within the app or from iOS Settings.

Is there a subscription or account?

No. OddTime is completely free with no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or accounts of any kind.

Children's privacy

OddTime is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data, there are no special considerations for younger users.

Changes to this policy

If anything about this policy changes in a future version of the app, this page will be updated to reflect that. The "Last updated" date at the bottom of this page will change accordingly.

For a free app with no data collection, significant changes are unlikely.
