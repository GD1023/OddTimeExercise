# OddTimeExercise
Small breaks. Big difference. (No gym. No excuses. Just quick, sensible breaks for your desk, couch, bed, or wherever you happen to be right now.)


You already know you should move more. The problem isn't motivation — it's the gap between knowing and doing.

OddTime fills those gaps.

It's a library of 87 guided exercises built for the moments most apps ignore: the 3 minutes between meetings, the 20 minutes on a plane, the last stretch before bed. Each one is short, specific, and actually designed for where you are — not some imaginary gym.

**How it works**

Pick where you are — at your desk, on the couch, traveling, lying in bed — and OddTime shows you exercises that make sense for that spot. Or pick a body area that needs attention. Or just tap Surprise Me and let the app pick for you.

There's no program to follow. No commitment to make. Just open it, do something, close it.

**What's inside**

- 87 exercises across 6 categories: Move, Mobility & Stretch, Strength & Activation, Eyes & Screen, Breathing & Reset, and Posture & Alignment
- Location-aware suggestions — exercises filtered by where you are and how much space you have
- Body area targeting — quickly find something for your neck, wrists, back, eyes, or anywhere else
- Step-by-step guidance with timing built in
- Surprise Me — one tap picks a random exercise from a random category
- Favorites — save the ones you keep coming back to

**Keep track**

OddTime tracks what you do so you can see it building up over time. Today's minutes, your streak, a weekly chart, and a full history of what you've done. Nothing fancy — just enough to keep you honest.

**Reminders that don't annoy you**

Set a time window — say 9am to 6pm — and a frequency, and OddTime will nudge you during the day. That's it. No badge spam, no daily streaks you'll feel guilty about breaking.

**Everything stays on your device**

No account. No subscription. No data leaving your phone. Everything is stored locally — your history, your preferences, your favorites.

OddTime is free. Try it during the odd time you have right now.
